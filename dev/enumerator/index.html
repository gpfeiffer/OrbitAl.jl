Coset Enumeration

Note

This is an on-demand module. Load it with using OrbitAl.enumerator. Example presentations are in OrbitAl.presentations.

A simple Todd-Coxeter coset enumerator, put together from three ingredients:

  • the orbit algorithm: each coset is finalized under each generator, while new cosets are being defined;
  • Union-Find (see OrbitAl.unionfind): cosets that turn out to be equal are merged in a forest on the cosets. Merging two cosets also merges their rows, which can cause further coincidences: Union-Find with consequences;
  • the variants of the relations: to find the image of a coset under a generator, the relations are rewritten so as to express the generator as a word in the others.

A presentation is a named tuple with fields gens (the generators 1:k, closed under inverses), invr (the inverse of each generator), rels (the relations, as pairs of words) and, optionally, sbgp (words generating a subgroup).

julia> using OrbitAl, OrbitAl.presentations, OrbitAl.enumerator

julia> T = coset_table(presentations.G12, presentations.G12.sbgp);

julia> T.active
24

julia> perms(coset_table(presentations.A2, presentations.A2.sbgp))
2-element Vector{Perm}:
 Perm([2, 1, 3])
 Perm([1, 3, 2])

julia> sizeOfGroup(PermGp(coset_table(presentations.A3, Vector{Int}[])))
24

Coset Tables

OrbitAl.enumerator.CosetTable — Type
CosetTable

A coset table under construction. Cosets are numbered 1, 2, 3, ...; next[x][s] is the image of coset x under generator s (0 if not yet known), parent is a Union-Find forest on the cosets (parent[x] == x if x is active), queue holds the pending coincidences, and words[x] is the word that defined coset x.

source
OrbitAl.enumerator.coset_table — Function
coset_table(genrel, sbgp)

Enumerate the cosets of the subgroup generated by the words sbgp in the group with presentation genrel (with fields gens, invr and rels). Returns a CosetTable; its active cosets are the cosets of the subgroup.

OrbitAl.enumerator.active_cosets — Method

The list of active cosets of T. Once the enumeration is complete, these are the cosets of the subgroup, and their number is T.active.
